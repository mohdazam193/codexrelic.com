IDENTITY.JOURNEY SRE • DEVOPS

From Frontline Support to Enterprise Reliability

I'm Mohd Azam, a DevOps & Site Reliability Engineer at Mitratech (Hyderabad), owning cloud platform reliability for enterprise legal & compliance SaaS.

My career began at ground zero after college in 2014—working overnight shifts in technical support and live operations command centers. Experiencing software failure from the user's perspective first shaped everything about how I architect, automate, and safeguard production systems today.

The Journey

1. Ground Zero: Electronic Arts (EA) Support

2013 – 2014

Started in global tech support (WWCE) during massive worldwide AAA launches including Battlefield 4 and Titanfall. Triaged live connection drops, multiplayer packet loss, NAT issues, and server outages under intense user pressure—learning firsthand how distributed services buckle and recover.

2. Amazon Operations & Command Center

2016 – 2019

Supported international marketplaces across IN, NA, and UK. Working on high-velocity initiatives like the CAP team, the MessageUS pilot, and contributing to Functional UAT testing for the Echo launch in India helped me understand early how to navigate and deliver within fast-moving pilot teams. Later moved to the Global Command Center as a Real-Time Analyst—monitoring live telemetry, queue health, and incident routing during Prime Day.

3. Enterprise SRE & Multi-Cloud: Mitratech

2020 – 2026

Owned production reliability for multi-tenant SaaS across Azure, AWS, Oracle Cloud, and Rackspace. Built full-stack observability (New Relic), led datacenter migrations, maintained 99.9%+ uptime, and automated deployment pipelines (50 min → 10 min; $4,300+/yr FinOps savings).

4. DevOps Engineer II: DevSecOps & GitOps

Present

Driving shift-left security (SAST/DAST in CI/CD), immutable Terraform infrastructure, container platforms, and open-source tooling for resilient software delivery.
