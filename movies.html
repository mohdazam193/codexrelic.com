Cinema Logs

Curated catalog of cinema recommendations. Hover cards for reviews and SRE analogies.

Interstellar (2014)

Sci-Fi / Space 10/10

Christopher Nolan's visual masterpiece. Hans Zimmer's organ score adds an almost gothic scale to the void of space.

SRE Analogy: Time Dilation Latency regression: 1 hour on Miller's planet is 7 years in callers' thread. Model your API timeouts!

Inception (2010)

Thriller / Heist 9.5/10

Architectural logic of dreams defined like code. City folding cinematography is stunning.

SRE Analogy: Virtualization Nested VMs (dream layers). A crash in a lower layer propagates up. Monitor base resources closely.

Blade Runner 2049 (2017)

Sci-Fi / Cyberpunk 9.8/10

Denis Villeneuve visual masterpiece. Slow burn detailing memory relics and what makes a soul.

SRE Analogy: Containers Replicants are containers instantiated from a base immutable image. Drift defines their separate lifecycles.

2001: A Space Odyssey (1968)

Philosophy / Classic 10/10

Kubrick's masterpiece. The monolith acts as a cosmic rulebook. HAL 9000 shows tragedy of system conflict.

SRE Analogy: Split-Brain HAL 9000 conflicting rules nervous breakdown. Partitioned cluster nodes thinking they are both master.
